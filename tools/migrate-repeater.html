MeshCore → MCRS repeater migration

Read the original repeater, keep a backup, flash or connect the replacement, then apply and verify its settings. All processing stays in this page; no libraries, servers or network requests.

Backups contain the full private key and other secrets. Keep the original repeater offline after cloning its identity. Writes are immediate and are not atomic; a failure can leave a partially migrated target.

1 · Read

Connect the original MeshCore repeater or load a saved backup. Close other serial terminals. Use desktop Chrome or Edge. Your backup stays in this browser.

No device connected.

Backups and the supplied admin password are saved automatically in this browser’s localStorage (plaintext) and restored when you return.

3 · Apply

Next, install MCRS on your repeater. Download your configuration backup first, then get the firmware for your board from the MCRS GitHub releases page and follow the installation instructions .

After installing MCRS, return here and connect the repeater to apply your saved configuration. Connecting reads its current settings and fills in retained values in Review.


MeshCore cannot export its admin password. Enter the password you want MCRS to use.

Supply an admin password before applying.

Settings are saved individually to MCRS’s configuration. Target-only settings and unrelated ACL/region entries are retained. Complete flat region tables and admin ACLs are migrated; other roles and region hierarchies need manual review. Identity and radio settings take effect after reboot. Reconnect and verify the active public key afterwards.
